## Entry

> Keep the mixtape she made you — twenty years on you'll still hum the third song at every red light, and only then will you finally hear why she chose it.

153 characters, one line.

## Why this angle

The field is crowded with father-and-phone lines and generic advice. This entry wins on a single concrete detail only its owner could know — a mixtape, a third song, red lights — and a turn at the end: the meaning of her choice only lands twenty years later. Warm, specific, no cliché.

## How to run

```bash
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (defaults to `/output`). Validates first: single line, 1–200 characters.
